Degen Golfers ’26Pick your crew

Up to four golfers, dressed the way they were in the photo, with stats built from each handicap and playing style. Tap a card to add or drop a golfer.

Swipe swing: put a finger high on the course view, pull it straight down for the backswing (further = more power), then push it straight back up past where you started. A smooth, quick push gives a pure strike; drifting left or right curves the ball, rushing it overhits, and a slow push loses power. Use the shape button for a draw, fade or low punch.
Classic 3-click: tap Swing to start the backswing, tap again to set power, then tap a third time as the marker crosses the yellow band for clean contact. Early or late contact curves the ball. Past 100% is overswing: more distance, much less control.
Aim: drag across the screen or use the arrows. The yellow ring shows your carry with no wind.
Beer: one per turn. Each beer loosens you up for your next 3 shots. Everyone has a secret limit, and going past it drops all your stats for the rest of the round.
Keyboard: space to swing, arrow keys to aim, Q and E to change clubs.
